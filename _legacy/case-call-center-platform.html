Call-Center Platform

Every call becomes a ticket, and no ticket gets lost — 24-hour coverage with escalation rules that match how the business actually works.

The challenge

The call that came in during lunch, got a promise, and vanished.

Every phone-heavy business shares that failure mode. When intake lives in memory and sticky notes, growth just means more dropped balls.

What we built

Before configuring anything, we map the real call flows: what people ask for, who should own each type of request, and what "urgent" actually means for this business. Escalation rules get written from that map — not from a generic template.

The platform makes every call a record. Intake is structured at the moment of contact, routing rules send each ticket to the right owner, and overflow rolls to trained specialists instead of voicemail. Calls are recorded and QA'd live.

How it runs day-to-day

Calls become structured tickets automatically. Routing fires by rule. Follow-ups are scheduled the moment a ticket is created. When volume spikes, overflow shifts to trained specialists — so the phone gets answered at 2 pm and at 2 am.

The platform is operational today and runs as a managed service: we operate the queue, staff the overflow, and report on volume, response times and resolution in plain numbers.

Results

Calls stopped being a liability. Every contact is captured, owned and visible in one queue, escalations follow the business's own rules, and spikes get absorbed by the overflow bench instead of burning out the team.

The final 10% is review — a human closes the loop on anything the rules can't, with live recordings and QA keeping quality honest.
