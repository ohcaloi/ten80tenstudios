Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a template document — have it reviewed by your attorney.

Ten80Ten ("we," "us," or "our") provides custom automation systems and trained virtual specialists to businesses. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain English, what information we collect through ten80ten.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. Short version: we collect only what we need to talk to you and do our work, and we never sell your data.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly. When you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you write in your message. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we receive your email address. When you become a client, we may collect additional business information needed to deliver our services — such as company details, billing information, and access credentials you choose to share with us for the systems we build and operate on your behalf.

Like most websites, we may also collect limited technical information automatically — such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location derived from your IP address — through cookies and analytics tools (see Section 4).

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, schedule and run Time-Back Audits, provide and improve our services, send you the newsletter you asked for, invoice for our work, and meet our legal obligations. We do not use your information for anything unrelated to running Ten80Ten.

3. What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not share client business data with other clients. We do not send marketing to addresses we obtained for service delivery without your permission.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working properly and to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use it (for example, which pages are most read). We may use third-party analytics providers for this purpose; these providers process usage data on our behalf under their own privacy safeguards. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings — the site will still work without them.

5. When we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — such as email, hosting, scheduling, and payment processors — and only to the extent needed for them to do their job. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety, or property. If Ten80Ten is ever part of a merger or acquisition, your information may transfer with the business, under the same protections described here.

6. Client systems and data

When we build and operate automation systems for you, we may have access to your business data and the personal data of your customers. That data belongs to you. We access it only to deliver the services you engaged us for, we require the same of our specialists, and on handover or termination we return or delete it per your instructions.

7. Data retention

We keep contact-form messages and correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and maintain business records, then delete them. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Client records are kept for the duration of the engagement plus the period required for tax and legal purposes.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information — including access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data as carefully as we protect our own.

9. Your rights and choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop emailing you. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@ten80ten.com — a real person will handle it.

10. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be flagged clearly on the site or by email to active clients.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@ten80ten.com. We reply within 24 hours.
