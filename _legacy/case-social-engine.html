Social Posting Engine

Roughly 70% of publishing automated end-to-end. Posts ship daily, on schedule — and the approval stays human, on purpose.

The challenge

Consistent publishing compounds. It's also the first thing that dies in a busy week.

The problem is never ideas. It's the pipeline: drafting, formatting for each channel, scheduling — and remembering to actually hit post.

What we built

The engine only works because the judgment came first: what the brand sounds like, what gets posted where, and what never goes out without a second look. Those rules were documented before anything was automated — they're the engine's guardrails.

Then we built an engine that runs the pipeline. Drafts queue themselves, channel formatting happens automatically, and posts ship on schedule across platforms. Roughly 70% of the process is automated end-to-end.

How it runs day-to-day

Drafts are generated and queued. Each one is formatted for its channel's quirks. The calendar fills itself and posts fire on schedule — daily, without a nightly scramble. Nobody babysits a posting queue; nothing waits in a drafts folder to be remembered.

This is the engine behind our own publishing, and the same install we offer clients as part of content operations: pipeline automated, voice documented, approvals human.

Results

Publishing became a system instead of a scramble. The calendar stays full, channels stay consistent, and the only human minutes spent are the ones that matter: reviewing and approving.
