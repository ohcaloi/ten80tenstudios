Real-Estate Data Portal

Comp and market reports that used to eat an afternoon now leave the office in minutes — while the CRM keeps itself current.

The challenge

Agents sell relationships. Their weeks were disappearing into report assembly.

Pulling comps. Formatting market updates. Exporting PDFs for listing presentations. Hours of copy-paste every week — work clients never see and never pay for.

What we built

Before a line of code, we sat with agents and traced exactly how a comp report gets made: where the data comes from, which numbers matter to a seller, and what the final document needs to look like to win a listing. That map became the portal's blueprint. Human judgment first — always.

Then we built a live data portal that does the assembling. A comp engine sits on top of market data covering all 50 states, trend views update themselves, and any report exports as a polished PDF or Excel file in one click. The CRM stays current behind the scenes — no record touched by hand.

How it runs day-to-day

Data feeds wired straight into the comp engine. Templates that format themselves. Exports that render in one click. CRM sync that files every record automatically. The repetitive 80% of report production now runs without anyone touching it.

This is our custom platform work at full depth — the tier we reach for when off-the-shelf tools genuinely can't carry a workflow. We design and build the system around how the business actually operates, and the client owns the result.

Results

The portal is live and in daily use. Report production moved from an afternoon of assembly to minutes of review, listing presentations go out the same day they're requested, and the CRM stopped drifting out of date.

The final 10% stays human: an agent reviews every report before it goes to a client.
