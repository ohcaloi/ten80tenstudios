Healthcare Clinic Automation

Scheduling, reminders, intake and billing follow-up on autopilot — so the front desk finally breathes.

Overview

Small practices lose money in the gaps — and it all lands on the front desk.

No-shows that were never reminded. Intake forms chased by phone. Invoices that slip for weeks. None of it is medical work, but it eats 15–20 admin hours per physician every week — and the front desk is drowning in it.

We install the automation layer underneath: appointment scheduling and reminder sequences, digital intake that fills the chart before the visit, and billing follow-up that never forgets. A trained specialist handles the exceptions — the reschedules, the edge cases, the humans.

The first 10% — mapping the patient journey

Every clinic runs differently, so we start by tracing one patient through the whole system: booking, confirmation, intake, visit, billing. The map shows exactly where hours leak — usually reminders dialed by hand and invoices chased from memory.

The 80%

// Clinical judgment stays with the clinic. We only take the grind.

Then reminders, intake and billing go on rails.

Reminder sequences fire on schedule. Intake forms arrive before the appointment and file themselves. Unpaid invoices get followed up automatically until they're resolved — or escalated to a human who owns the exception queue.

It's a standard 10/80/10 automation install, tuned for practices: we work inside the tools the clinic already uses wherever possible, document every SOP, and place a specialist who runs the system day to day.

The final 10% is the clinic's: review the outcomes, keep the judgment calls. Staff time moves from chasing paperwork to greeting patients.
